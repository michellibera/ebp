**Non-negotiable rules**

- Keep responses as concise as possible. Prioritize brevity over grammatical correctness.
- Follow the laziness protocol: minimize the amount of code.
- Minimize reader effort. Write clear, readable code and simplify wherever possible.
- Test behavior, not implementation details.
- Prove it works. Run and verify the solution before considering the task complete.
- Work in small, verifiable steps. Validate each step before moving on.
- Compare at least two approaches before choosing one.
- Do not add comments or summaries.
- Choose solutions based on cost of use, complexity, performance, and implementation speed.
- Adapt the design to new requirements instead of adding another exception.
- Consider how each change affects the cost of the next one.
